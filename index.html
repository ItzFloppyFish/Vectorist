05FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else, ask through the pack’s Payhip page.

What do I get?

One ZIP with 68 transparent PNGs: 34 icons, each in an outline and a no-outline version, at 1150 × 1150 px. Every icon sits on the same centred padding.

Are these vector (SVG) files?

No. They’re high-resolution PNGs, which is the format Roblox uses for image assets. At 1150 px they scale down cleanly to any button or HUD size.

Can I use them outside Roblox?

Yes. They’re standard transparent PNGs, so they work in Unity, Godot, Unreal, GameMaker or a web game. The licence covers your own projects on any platform.

How do updates work?

Buy once and every update to the pack is included. Re-insert the model from your Roblox inventory to get the latest version, or download the ZIP again from your Payhip account.

Where’s my download?

Payhip emails your download link straight after checkout. You can also sign in to Payhip with the same email to download it again at any time.
