What format are the icons?

Transparent PNGs at a high resolution, so they scale down cleanly to any button or HUD size. Each icon comes with an outline and a no-outline version.

Can I use them outside Roblox?

Yes. They work in Unity, Godot, Unreal, GameMaker or a web game. Use them in your own games on any platform.

How do updates work?

Buy a pack once and every update to it is included. Re-insert the model from your Roblox inventory to get the latest version, or download the files again from your Payhip account.

Where’s my download?

Payhip emails your download link straight after checkout. You can also sign in to Payhip with the same email to download it again.
