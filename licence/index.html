§Licence

Icon licence.

The short version: use the icons in your own games, don’t share or sell the files.

What you can do

  • Use the icons in your own experiences and games, on Roblox or any other engine.
  • Use the outline and no-outline versions in any combination.
  • Receive every future update to a pack you’ve bought, at no extra cost.

What you can’t do

  • Re-upload the icons anywhere, including the Roblox Creator Store or any asset marketplace.
  • Resell the icons, on their own or as part of another pack, kit, template or game file.
  • Share or redistribute the files to anyone else, whether free or paid.

Updates

Re-insert the model from your Roblox inventory to get the latest version, or download the files again from your Payhip account.

Questions

Not sure whether your use is covered? Ask before you use the icons. Reach us through the pack’s Payhip page.
